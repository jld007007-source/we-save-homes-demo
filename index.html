8 Ways to Stop Foreclosure

A clear overview of common paths. Jesse helps you understand the landscape and connects you with licensed attorneys when legal work is needed.

1
Talk early
Contact your lender before missed payments stack up.
2
Repayment plan
Catch up overdue amounts over time.
3
Forbearance
Temporary pause or reduction (lender programs vary).
4
Loan options
Refinance via licensed lenders — Jesse refers, he doesn’t close loans.
5
Deed in lieu
Voluntary transfer to the lender in some cases.
6
Short sale
Sell with lender approval when equity is gone.
7
Bankruptcy counsel
Chapter 7/13 is attorney-only — referral to licensed counsel.
8
Sell & restart
A planned sale can stop the clock better than auction.

Important: We Save Homes provides real-estate guidance and foreclosure-prevention education. We are not a mortgage lender or Nevada “covered service provider” for loan modification / foreclosure-rescue services. Legal and lending work is handled by independently licensed attorneys and lenders.
